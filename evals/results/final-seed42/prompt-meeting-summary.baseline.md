Here's a prompt you can use with ChatGPT:

---

**Prompt:**

> I'm going to paste my raw meeting notes below. Please summarize them into the following format:
>
> 1. **Meeting overview** — 1-2 sentences on purpose/context (if evident from the notes)
> 2. **Key discussion points** — bullet list of main topics covered
> 3. **Decisions made** — bullet list of any firm decisions or conclusions
> 4. **Action items** — bullet list in the format: `[Owner] — [Task] — [Deadline, if mentioned]`
> 5. **Open questions / follow-ups** — anything unresolved or needing further discussion
>
> Keep it concise — use plain language, no filler, and don't invent details that aren't in the notes. If a section has nothing relevant, write "None noted."
>
> Here are my notes:
> [PASTE YOUR MEETING NOTES HERE]

---

Just paste your actual notes in place of the bracketed placeholder before sending it.
