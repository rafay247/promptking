**Pick: GoCabby** — short, friendly, available-sounding, and instantly readable as a cab app.

Here are 10 strong candidates grouped by naming strategy:

**Descriptive (clear what it does)**
- **GoCabby** — playful, easy to say, implies a helpful "your own cabby"
- **RideNow** — direct, conveys speed/urgency
- **CabFleet** — signals scale/reliability, good if you have many drivers
- **QuickCab** — leads with the core value prop (speed)

**Evocative (feeling/benefit, not literal)**
- **Wayfare** — evokes journey + fare, has a premium feel
- **Hailo** — "hail a cab," rolls off the tongue (check for trademark conflicts — a UK cab app used this name previously)
- **Zephyr Rides** — suggests fast, smooth travel
- **Commutly** — modern, implies everyday reliability for commuters

**Coined/invented (brandable, easiest to trademark)**
- **Cabzy** — short, app-store-friendly, easy to pronounce globally
- **Tuko** — invented, short, works well as a logo/wordmark, no baggage from existing meanings

**Shortlist:**
1. **GoCabby** — best balance of clarity + personality, strong for a consumer-facing app
2. **Cabzy** — cleanest for branding/logo work, very "app store" native
3. **Wayfare** — best if you want a more premium/upmarket positioning
