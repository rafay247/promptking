**Name ideas for a cab booking app**

**Descriptive** (clear about what it does)
1. **RideNow** — immediate, action-oriented, easy to say
2. **CabGo** — short, punchy, reads well as an app icon wordmark
3. **QuickCab** — leans into speed as the core promise
4. **HailWay** — modern twist on "hailing a cab"
5. **RideLink** — implies connection between rider and driver

**Evocative** (feeling/experience over literal function)
6. **Fareway** — plays on "fare" + "freeway," forward motion
7. **Zippo** *(check conflicts — close to the lighter brand)* → alt: **Zypp**
8. **Driftly** — smooth, low-friction ride feel
9. **Arrivo** — Latin-root, suggests reliable arrival
10. **Roam** — simple, open, suits city + intercity use

**Coined / brandable** (invented, highly trademarkable)
11. **Cabsy** — friendly, diminutive, good for a consumer app
12. **Tryp** — short for "trip," modern spelling trend (Uber/Lyft-style)

**Top 3 shortlist**
- **Cabsy** — most ownable and memorable; coined words clear trademark/domain searches far more easily than descriptive ones.
- **RideNow** — strongest immediate clarity for new users scanning an app store.
- **Tryp** — best if you want a sleek, Gen-Z-leaning brand feel.

**Recommended pick: Cabsy** — it's distinctive enough to trademark and get a clean .com/app-store slot, while still sounding approachable and obviously ride-related once paired with a car icon or tagline ("Your ride, your way").

**Next step:** before committing, check (1) domain availability (cabsy.com/.app), (2) App Store/Play Store name collisions, (3) social handles, and (4) a basic trademark search in your country/category — coined names like Cabsy and Tryp clear this faster than descriptive ones like RideNow or CabGo.

Assumed: general consumer ride-hailing app, no specific city/region or brand tone given — skewed toward friendly/modern rather than premium or budget-focused.
