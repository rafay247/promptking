A few tips for narrowing down:
- Check domain/app-store availability and that it doesn't clash with existing rideshare trademarks (Uber, Ola, Lyft, Grab, etc.).
- One or two syllables are easiest to say and remember for a "hail a ride" use case.
- If you're targeting a specific city/country, a locally-flavored name (like a regional word for "fast" or "ride") can stand out more than a generic English name.
